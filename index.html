            How to progress
            • Weeks 1–2: learn the moves with light weights. End each set with 3–4 reps left in the tank.
            • Then, when you hit the top of the rep range on every set, add 2.5 kg (upper body) or 5 kg (legs).
            • Rest 2–3 min after the first lift, 60–90 s after the others.
            • Every 6th week, do the same plan with about half the sets to recover.
            • Ask a trainer to check your squat, deadlift and bench form in week one.
            • Sore muscles are normal. Sharp joint pain means stop that exercise.
